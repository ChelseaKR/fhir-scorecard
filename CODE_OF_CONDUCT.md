# Code of conduct

FHIR Scorecard is a public-interest measurement project. Participation here should make its data
more accurate, its methods more transparent, and its effects less harmful.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific, evidence-led, and respectful when questioning a grade, attribution, or method.
- Assume that mistakes can be corrected. Critique the record or the method, not the person or
  organization reporting it.
- Protect private information. Do not post credentials, patient data, security-sensitive server
  details, or personal contact information in an issue or pull request.
- Respect removal and correction requests. This project measures public surfaces and has no
  interest in an adversarial relationship with the organizations it observes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disclosure of private information, and
attempts to use this project to target people or organizations are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For conduct concerns, email ckellyreif@gmail.com. Reports will be handled privately. The maintainer
may edit or remove contributions, lock conversations, or temporarily or permanently exclude a
participant when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.

Security issues in the tooling should be reported through the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public issue.
